The Story of Aaron — Founder, CEO & MD of Aaron Garments

Our True Origin • From 60–65 Sq. Ft. to Regional Powerhouse

"Most people stay in the lane they studied for. Aaron, a BSc Nursing graduate, traded a steady hospital career for his true passion—men’s fashion—starting out of a tiny 60–65 sq. ft. room in Chennai."

Armed with the discipline and care he learned in healthcare, Aaron mastered retail from the ground up: knowing every customer, curating trend-forward styles early, and making every square foot count.

Over the last decade, that single room has grown into Aaron Garments—a thriving network of 15+ retail stores spanning 22,000+ sq. ft. across Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh, backed by a massive 10,000 sq. ft. Central Distribution Centre (DC), corporate office, and wholesale hub in Chennai.

Today, we continue to grow on three simple pillars: sharp fashion forecasting, strong worldwide & pan-India sourcing, and empowering our store teams to deliver the best in modern menswear.
